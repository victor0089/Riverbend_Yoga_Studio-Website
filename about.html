Accessibility and participation

We aim to offer a respectful environment where students can ask for seated variations, extra explanation, or a slower pace without pressure. If you have mobility, sensory, or other access needs, contact us before visiting so we can discuss the available space and options together.

Students may take breaks or opt out of a pose at any time. Please ask an instructor before class if you are uncertain about suitability.

Studio policies and expectations

  • Arrive a little early for your first visit to discuss questions.
  • Speak respectfully and allow others a quiet practice space.
  • Tell the instructor if you prefer no hands-on adjustments.
  • Pause when needed; yoga is not a competition.
  • Ask about attendance, cancellation, and class-pack terms before booking.
  • Discuss health concerns with a qualified professional as appropriate.

Group classes

Gentle Yoga

A slower-paced class with straightforward instructions, stretching, and optional chair-based or supported variations. A welcoming choice for beginners and older adults.

Vinyasa Flow

A moving sequence that connects breath and posture. Instructors suggest options for different energy levels; students are encouraged to pause or rest as needed.

Restorative Yoga

Quiet, supported poses and relaxed breathing offer time to unwind. Especially helpful for busy schedules and those seeking a less physically demanding practice.

Private yoga sessions

One-to-one sessions allow time to discuss comfort, goals, and preferred movement options. Contact us to ask about introductory sessions, scheduling, and how we can adapt a practice to your needs.

Flexible pricing options

Riverbend offers several ways to participate. Current prices and availability are shared when you inquire; there is no online checkout.

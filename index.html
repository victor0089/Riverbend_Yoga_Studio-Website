Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Movement and calm for every body

Our mission is to make yoga approachable through supportive teaching, mindful breathing, and choices that respect different abilities and goals.

New to yoga? You belong here.

You do not need special flexibility or previous experience. Our instructors explain poses, offer seated or lower-intensity options, and invite students to rest whenever needed.

Explore beginner-friendly classes

Featured class: Gentle Flow

Gentle Flow combines unhurried movement and simple breathing cues. Beginners, returning students, and anyone seeking a softer pace are welcome. Ask about modifications before your first class.
